# Brief

## Who we are

Verspui is the family office of Surekha (Cath) Verspui and Erik Verspui, in Phuket and Bangkok. Surekha is a Thai citizen; Erik is Dutch. On the site she is always Surekha Verspui. We invest our own money and have made twenty private investments since 2024.

## What we offer

We become the majority Thai shareholder in businesses started by founders from abroad, and we take an active part: our own money in, a seat on the board, and hands-on help where we are strong. We are long-term owners, not a fund with a deadline to sell.

## Who the site is for

1. Founders from abroad starting a business in Thailand that needs a Thai majority shareholder.
2. Companies whose current Thai shareholder plays no real part and that want one who does. Since 2026 the Thai authorities have been checking whether Thai shareholders genuinely fund and control their shares, so many founders are restructuring.
3. Real estate and hospitality businesses in Phuket: developers, hotels and rental operators.
4. Consumer, wellness, fitness, food and software companies.

They are usually experienced business owners in Phuket or Bangkok, often reading on a phone. They are sceptical, and nervous about two things: staying legal, and handing majority ownership to people they don't know.

## What the site must make them believe, in this order

1. These are real, named, accountable people with a track record in Thailand.
2. The partnership is genuine, and that is what keeps it legal.
3. They bring something specific.
4. Starting a conversation is easy and confidential.

## The one action

Send us an email.

## Structure

One page, plus Notes.

1. Header: wordmark, section links
2. Hero: headline, intro, email link
3. Approach
4. What we bring
5. Track record
6. Partners
7. Who we work with
8. Notes: the latest three (hidden until at least one is published)
9. Contact
10. Footer

Other pages: `/notes/` (listing), `/notes/[slug]/` (articles), `/privacy/`, and a 404 page.

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. "We", short sentences, no hype, no superlatives. Say what we do, not how good we are. The reader is smart and busy.

## Never on the site

- Deal terms, prices, or any percentage beyond the word "majority"
- Anything that sounds like a service for holding shares
- Companies that haven't launched, by name
- Lists of crypto or token investments
- Photos of people, stock photos, AI-generated images, company logos
- Testimonials or quotes we haven't supplied
- The phrases banned in `CLAUDE.md`
